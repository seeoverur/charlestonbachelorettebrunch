Private Party Restaurant Bookings in Charleston

How to book a private room or a full restaurant buyout in Charleston, SC for a bachelorette, birthday, or rehearsal dinner.

Charleston restaurants are built for celebrations, but weekend tables for a big group fill up fast. If your party is 12 people or more, booking a private dining room or a semi-private space is usually easier than trying to squeeze everyone into a regular reservation.

Private room, semi-private, or buyout?

A private dining room is a closed space just for your group, which is best for toasts, speeches, and gifts. A semi-private area is a reserved section of the main dining room, so you keep the restaurant's energy without needing a big minimum. A full buyout gives you the whole restaurant, which works for large rehearsal dinners or when you want a DJ or a set program.

What to expect when you book

  • Food and beverage minimum: most private spaces require a minimum spend, which is often higher on Friday and Saturday nights.
  • Set menu: larger groups usually order from a fixed family-style or prix-fixe menu instead of the full menu.
  • Deposit: expect a deposit or a card on file to hold the date, plus a cancellation window.
  • Service charge: many restaurants add an automatic gratuity for private events, so check before you tip again.

Tips for booking a private party in Charleston

  • Reach out 4 to 8 weeks ahead for spring and fall weekends, which is peak wedding and bachelorette season.
  • Use the restaurant's private events or group dining form instead of the regular reservation line.
  • Ask about decorations, a cake or dessert fee, and whether you can bring your own playlist.
  • Choose a restaurant close to your rental or the next stop, like Upper King Street, so the group can walk.
  • Consider a daytime brunch party. Private rooms are often easier to book and have lower minimums before dinner service.
