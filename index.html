Why brunch is the main event of a Charleston bachelorette

A Charleston bachelorette weekend usually runs on late nights on Upper King Street, so the brunch the next morning ends up being the meal everyone remembers. It is where the bride gets her toast, where the group photos get taken, and where the plan for the rest of the day comes together.

Charleston is one of the best brunch cities in the South. You can go Lowcountry classic with shrimp and grits and biscuits, go loud with a live gospel band, go seafood with oysters and a lobster-topped Bloody Mary, or keep it casual with coffee and a quick bite before a harbor cruise. Every spot on this list is on or near the Charleston peninsula, so your group can walk or take a short rideshare from most downtown Airbnbs and hotels.

The 6 best Charleston brunch spots for a bachelorette party

Weekend brunch in Charleston books up fast, especially in spring and fall. For groups of 8 or more, reserve two to four weeks ahead and confirm current hours directly with the restaurant before you lock in your itinerary.

#1Local Pick

Sweet Palm Coffee & Food

471 King St · Upper King · $

CoffeeGrab & goWalkable

Sweet Palm is the easy-win brunch for the morning after a big night on Upper King. It's a coffee and food café right on King Street, so the group can roll out of a nearby Airbnb, grab coffee, breakfast, a wrap, or a dirty soda, and be fueled up without waiting on a two-hour reservation. It also works as your pre-activity stop before a boat cruise or a shopping walk down King.

Bachelorette tip: Use Sweet Palm on the morning you have an activity booked. Save the long sit-down brunch for the other day.
Visit Sweet Palm
#2

Halls Chophouse

434 King St · Upper King · $$$

Gospel brunchLive musicUpscale

Halls Chophouse hosts a Sunday gospel brunch with a live band, which makes it one of the most memorable bachelorette brunches in Charleston. The menu leans steakhouse-meets-brunch with dishes like crab cake benedict and a signature sticky bun, and the room has real energy.

Bachelorette tip: Book it for your Sunday farewell brunch. It's a high note to send everyone home on.
#3

The Darling Oyster Bar

513 King St · Upper King · $$

SeafoodBloody MarysPhoto-ready

The Darling is the brunch pick for a seafood-loving bride. Think oysters, lobster benedict, and a Bloody Mary topped with a lobster claw that practically begs to be on Instagram. The bright, classic oyster-bar room sits right in the middle of the King Street action.

Bachelorette tip: Order a round of oysters for the table while you wait on entrées. It's an easy shared toast moment.
#4

Church and Union

32B N Market St · French Quarter / Market · $$

Former churchMimosasBig groups

Brunch inside a former church is a fun, slightly cheeky backdrop for a bachelorette. Church and Union is known for its big brunch plates, including a breakfast special that bundles in a mimosa or Bloody Mary, and it's steps from the City Market for shopping afterward.

Bachelorette tip: Pair it with a stroll through the City Market after brunch since you're already right there.
#5

Poogan's Porch

72 Queen St · French Quarter · $$

Southern classicHistoric houseBiscuits

Set in a Victorian house on Queen Street, Poogan's Porch is one of Charleston's oldest restaurants and the definition of Southern charm. Go for the buttermilk biscuits, fried chicken and waffles, and she-crab soup. It's perfect for a bride who wants the classic Charleston experience.

Bachelorette tip: Ask for porch or upstairs seating when you reserve. The house itself makes a great group photo.
#6

Frannie & The Fox

Hotel Emeline · French Quarter / Ansonborough · $$

Hotel brunchWood-firedChic

Frannie & The Fox is the stylish, wood-fired restaurant inside Hotel Emeline, with a relaxed courtyard feel and a strong weekend brunch. It's a favorite pick for bachelorette groups that want something polished but not stuffy.

Bachelorette tip: A good middle ground when half the group wants fancy and the other half wants casual.

Brunch + Activities

3 things to do after your Charleston bachelorette brunch

Brunch sets up the day. These three activities pair naturally with a downtown brunch and keep the whole group together between brunch and dinner.

Activity 1

Rainbow Row & The Battery Photo Walk

East Bay St & Waterfront · Free

PhotosWalkableFree

Walk off brunch with the most photographed stretch of Charleston. Rainbow Row's pastel houses on East Bay Street make the perfect backdrop for matching-outfit group photos, and it's a short stroll on to The Battery and the harbor waterfront. It's an easy walk from the French Quarter brunch spots like Poogan's Porch and Church and Union.

Tip: Remember these are private homes, so keep the group on the sidewalk and off the steps.
Activity 2

Tiki Tours of Charleston: Party Boat Charter

Departs Brittlebank Park · Charleston Harbor & Ashley River

BYOBPrivate charterUp to 32 guests

A harbor boat cruise is the classic Charleston bachelorette activity, and it's the perfect follow-up to brunch. Tiki Tours runs a tiki party boat that fits large groups, with private charters so the bride gets her own playlist and the whole crew on the water together. Grab brunch, then head to the dock for an afternoon cruise.

Tip: Spring and fall weekends book out weeks ahead, so reserve the boat before you finalize brunch times.
Activity 3

King Street Shopping & Holst + Lee Charm Bar

King Street & 17 State St, French Quarter

ShoppingKeepsakeGroup activity

King Street is Charleston's main shopping street, lined with boutiques that are great for picking up a going-out outfit for that night. For a group activity with a keepsake, Holst + Lee in the French Quarter hosts private charm bar parties where everyone builds their own charm necklace.

Tip: Start at a brunch spot on Lower King and shop your way north toward Upper King for the evening.

How to plan a Charleston bachelorette brunch

Book early for groups of 8+

Many Charleston brunch spots cap reservations for large parties or require a set menu. Call the restaurant directly for groups over 8, and ask about split checks before you go.

Pick your brunch by the day, not just the menu

On Saturday, you'll probably have an activity after brunch, so pick something walkable to your next stop. On Sunday, choose a longer, bigger brunch like the Halls gospel brunch as your send-off before everyone heads to the airport.

Stay close to King Street

Most of the brunch spots on this list sit on or near King Street, Meeting Street, or the French Quarter. Staying on the peninsula means the group can walk to brunch and skip rideshare surges on busy weekends.

Plan around the weather

Charleston summers are hot and humid, and afternoon storms are common. Rooftop and patio brunches are best in spring and fall. In summer, go early or book indoor seating.

Bring the bride details

A sash, a veil, or a small table decoration makes the brunch photos feel like a celebration. Tell the restaurant it's a bachelorette when you book, since many will seat you somewhere more festive.
