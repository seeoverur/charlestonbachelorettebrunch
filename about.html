About the guide

About Charleston Bachelorette Brunch

A focused, local guide to one thing: finding the right brunch for a bachelorette party in Charleston, South Carolina.

Why this site exists

Most Charleston bachelorette guides try to cover everything at once, like bars, boats, rentals, and decor, and brunch ends up as a short list at the bottom. But for most bachelorette groups, brunch is the meal that anchors the weekend. It's where the bride gets toasted, where the group photos happen, and where the rest of the day gets planned.

Charleston Bachelorette Brunch was built to answer that question well. We look at Charleston brunch spots the way a group planner does: Can they seat a group? Is it walkable from King Street? Are the drinks worth a toast? Is the room photo-worthy? What should you do right after?

How we choose brunch spots

Every restaurant on the list is on or near the Charleston peninsula and serves brunch or an all-day breakfast menu. We favor spots that:

  • Work well for groups of 6 to 15+
  • Offer brunch cocktails like mimosas and Bloody Marys, or a standout coffee program
  • Are walkable from King Street, the French Quarter, and downtown rentals
  • Pair easily with a bachelorette activity afterward, like a harbor cruise, a Rainbow Row photo walk, or King Street shopping

We update the guide as Charleston's brunch scene changes. Restaurants open, close, and change menus often, so always confirm hours and reservation policies directly with each business before you book.

Planning the rest of your weekend

This site sticks to brunch and the activities that fit right around it. For a complete list of bachelorette activities in Charleston, including nightlife, boat parties, beach days, and classes, visit Charleston Bachelorette Activities.
