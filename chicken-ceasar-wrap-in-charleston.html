Chicken Caesar Wrap in Charleston

Somewhere between brunch and dinner the group needs lunch, and nobody wants another long table. The chicken Caesar wrap at Sweet Palm Coffee & Food on King Street is the quick, lighter meal that keeps a bachelorette Saturday moving.

See the Sweet Palm Menu Back to Home

The wrap that became the house favorite

At Sweet Palm Coffee & Food, 471 King Street, the chicken Caesar wrap started as one item on a cafe menu and turned into the thing regulars order most. It is built with grilled chicken, crisp romaine, shaved parmesan and Caesar dressing in a toasted flour tortilla, and it is made when you order it.

It is a full meal. The portion is large enough to split or to save half for later, which matters on a day with dinner reservations at eight.

There is a combo here too: a personal-size Boca Chicken Caesar wrap with a canned soda, bottled water or iced latte, listed at $10.99 on the cafe's menu.

Three Caesar wraps to choose from

The original was popular enough that the cafe added variations. Ask at the counter which are on the board the day you visit.

Where it fits in a bachelorette weekend

Lunch between shopping and getting ready

King Street shopping runs right past the door. Stop in mid-afternoon, eat at a table or take the wraps back to the rental, and the group is fed without losing an hour.

Arrival-day food

Flights land at different times and nobody is ready for a group dinner at 2 p.m. Wraps for whoever has arrived solves the first meal of the trip.

Packed for the boat or the beach

A wrap handles a cooler bag better than most lunch food. Pick them up on the way to a harbor charter or a Folly Beach afternoon.

Ordering for the whole group

Sweet Palm names its chicken Caesar wraps as a bulk favorite and takes large takeout orders for bridal groups. Call or email ahead so everything is made fresh for one pickup. For single orders, use online pickup.

Mornings are covered too

The same counter serves the classic breakfast burrito, which is the early version of this stop.
