The Classic Breakfast Burrito in Charleston

Not every bachelorette morning needs a two-hour reservation. When the group wants real food fast, the classic egg and cheese burrito at Sweet Palm Coffee & Food on King Street does the job, and it travels well to wherever the day is headed.

See the Sweet Palm Menu Back to Home

A simple burrito done right on King Street

Sweet Palm Coffee & Food is a quick-serve cafe at 471 King Street, in the middle of the Upper King blocks where most bachelorette groups spend their nights. The classic is the straightforward one on the menu: eggs and melted cheese rolled in a warm flour tortilla, made in house when you order it.

That simplicity is the point. It is easy on a stomach that stayed out late, it suits the friend who does not want anything spicy, and nobody has to study a menu. The kitchen takes changes at the counter, so anyone who wants more can add to it.

The burrito also comes as a combo: a personal-size egg and cheese burrito with an iced latte and one flavor of your choice, listed at $10.99 on the cafe's menu.

Why it works for a bachelorette group

A sit-down brunch is the main event. This is the breakfast for the other morning, or the one before the main event.

Reason 1

No reservation, no wait list

Counter service

Weekend brunch tables downtown book weeks out for parties of eight or more. Here the group walks in, orders at the counter and eats within minutes.

Bachelorette tip: Send two people ahead to order while everyone else finishes getting ready.
Reason 2

Walkable from Upper King stays

471 King St

Most downtown rentals and hotels near Marion Square are a short walk away, so there is no rideshare to organize before anyone has had coffee.

Bachelorette tip: It is on the way to King Street shopping, so breakfast and the first stop of the day are the same walk.
Reason 3

Easy to carry to the next stop

Grab and go

A wrapped burrito holds together in a tote bag or on the ride to the dock. That makes it the practical breakfast before a boat charter, a beach day or a photo walk.

Bachelorette tip: Order ahead for pickup on mornings with a fixed departure time.

How to order for the whole house

Make it your own at the counter

The classic is eggs and cheese. Ask at the register about adding a protein or other extras, and the same fillings can be served as a bowl for anyone skipping the tortilla.

Pair it with a drink

The iced latte combo is the fast choice. The Sweet Palm Latte is the house signature, and there is matcha, iced tea and a list of dirty sodas for the friends who do not drink coffee.

Order ahead for a big group

For ten or more burritos, place the order in advance so everything is hot and ready together. Sweet Palm takes large takeout orders by phone, email or its contact form, and one person can pick up for the house.

Save room for brunch if you have it booked

If a sit-down brunch is on the schedule later that day, split burritos between two people. If lunch is more the plan, the chicken Caesar wrap from the same counter is the midday version of this stop.
