Why book brunch catering in Charleston

Weekend brunch reservations in downtown Charleston are hard to get for big groups, and the morning after a night on Upper King is rarely when a bachelorette crew wants to get dressed and wait for a table. Brunch catering flips that around. The food comes to you, the group stays together at the rental, and you can set up a mimosa bar, decorate the table, and take all the bride photos you want without anyone rushing you.

Catering also works well when your group is larger than most restaurants can seat, when you're staying outside downtown on Folly Beach, Isle of Palms, James Island, or in Mount Pleasant, or when you have a boat or activity booked right after brunch.

How to order brunch catering for a group

Order at least 2–3 days ahead

Most brunch caterers need notice, and weekend slots fill up in spring and fall wedding season. Place your order before you travel so it's one less thing to handle once you land.

Plan food for the headcount, plus a little extra

Many group trays are sized for about 10 people. For a group of 12 to 15, order two trays of the main items and add a pastry or fruit platter so nobody goes hungry.

Set up your own mimosa bar

Pick up champagne, orange juice, and a couple of fruit juices the day before, then set out flutes, fresh berries, and a sign for the bride. It's the easiest way to make a catered brunch feel like a party.

Confirm delivery or pickup details

Share the full rental address, gate or parking instructions, and a contact number. If pickup is the only option, send one person early while the rest of the group gets ready.

Schedule it around your activity

If you have a boat charter or tour booked, time the brunch delivery for about two hours before so there's time to eat, take photos, and get going.
